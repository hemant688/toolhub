Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does resizing image reduce image quality?

If aspect ratio is locked and appropriate output resolution is chosen, your image retains razor-sharp quality.

What is the benefit of WEBP format?

WEBP offers up to 30% smaller file sizes than JPG while preserving maximum clarity, perfect for fast website loading.

Is my photo uploaded to any online server?

No. All calculations, pixel resizing, and canvas rendering happen inside your web browser.
