Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does rotating a PDF change its content quality?

No! Rotating only updates the page viewport angle in PDF structure. Content quality remains 100% identical.

Can I rotate single pages inside a multi-page PDF?

Yes, each rendered thumbnail card has its own rotation button to let you fix individual pages.

Is there a limit on how many times I can rotate a PDF?

No, you can rotate pages as many times as you like before saving.
